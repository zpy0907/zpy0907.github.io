Our History

Peach Ball originated from ancient Chinese folk games and has evolved over thousands of years into a systematic sport.

2015

Association Establishment

Founded by 10 Peach Ball enthusiasts, officially registered with Beijing Sports Bureau

2017

First National Peach Ball Championship

Successfully organized the first national championship with 32 participating teams

2019

Peach Ball in University Curriculum

Collaborated with universities to include Peach Ball in physical education curriculum

2021

International Peach Ball Exchange Conference

Hosted the first international conference with representatives from 15 countries

2023

CPA 10th Anniversary Celebration

Celebrated 10th anniversary with over 5,000 members, becoming one of the most influential sports associations

About CPA

The Chinese Peach Ball Association is dedicated to promoting and developing Peach Ball sports, preserving traditional Chinese sports culture, and encouraging nationwide fitness activities.

Our goal is to make Peach Ball a beloved sport for everyone.
